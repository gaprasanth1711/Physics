📖 NCERT Class 11 Physics: Circular Motion

1. Bending of a Cyclist (NCERT Section 5.10)

When a cyclist rounds a curved path of radius r at speed v, a centripetal force is necessary. If the cyclist remains upright, friction provides the centripetal force, which exerts an overturning torque about the center of mass. To prevent toppling, the cyclist leans inward at an angle θ with the vertical.

Key Equations (Cyclist):

Vertical Equilibrium: R cos θ = mg
Centripetal Requirement: R sin θ = mv² / r
Dividing: tan θ = v² / (rg) ⇒ θ = arctan(v² / rg)
Maximum Safe Speed without Skidding: v_max = √(μ_s · r · g)

2. Motion of a Car on a Level Road

On a horizontal level road, normal reaction balances gravity (N = mg). The only horizontal force acting towards the center of curvature is the static friction f_s between the tires and the asphalt.

Key Equations (Level Road):

Centripetal Force: f_s = mv² / r
Limiting Friction Condition: f_s ≤ μ_s N = μ_s mg
Maximum Safe Speed: v_max = √(μ_s · r · g)
Note: If v > v_max, static friction is exceeded, and the vehicle skids outward!

3. Motion of a Car on a Banked Road

To reduce dependence on friction and prevent tire wear at high speeds, roads are banked (outer edge raised relative to inner edge) by angle θ.

Key Equations (Banked Road):

(a) Optimum Speed (Zero Lateral Friction, f = 0):
N sin θ = mv₀² / r  |  N cos θ = mg ⇒ v₀ = √(rg · tan θ)

(b) Maximum Safe Speed (Friction down the incline):
v_max = √[ rg · (μ_s + tan θ) / (1 - μ_s tan θ) ]

(c) Minimum Safe Speed (Friction up the incline, if tan θ > μ_s):
v_min = √[ rg · (tan θ - μ_s) / (1 + μ_s tan θ) ] (If tan θ ≤ μ_s, v_min = 0)

🎯 CBSE Class 11 Exam Challenge Problems

1. A cyclist negotiating a sharp circular curve of radius r = 20 m at speed v = 14 m/s (g = 9.8 m/s²). What is the lean angle θ with the vertical to maintain balance?
2. A car is traveling on a flat circular track of radius 50 m. The coefficient of static friction is μ_s = 0.5 (g = 9.8 m/s²). What is the maximum speed without skidding?
3. A highway curve of radius 100 m is banked at 15°. At what optimum speed (v₀) can a car round the turn with ZERO wear on tires (no lateral friction required)? (g = 9.8 m/s²)
