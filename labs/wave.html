Standing Waves & String Harmonics Simulation

Frequently Asked Conceptual & Viva Questions

What is the distance between a node and the adjacent antinode?

Exactly one-quarter of a wavelength (lambda / 4).

How does string frequency change if tension is quadrupled?

Since f is proportional to sqrt(T), quadrupling tension doubles the frequency.
