Key Formulas & Equations

$$Z = \sqrt{R^2 + (X_L - X_C)^2},\quad X_L = \omega L,\; X_C = \frac{1}{\omega C}$$
$$\omega_r = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}},\quad f_r = \frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}$$
$$Q = \frac{\omega_r L}{R} = \frac{1}{R}\sqrt{\frac{L}{C}}$$

Frequently Asked Conceptual & Viva Questions

What is the power factor of a series LCR circuit at resonance?

At resonance XL = XC so Z = R and phase angle phi = 0, giving maximum power factor cos(phi) = 1.

How does decreasing resistance R affect resonance sharpness?

Lower R raises peak current and increases Quality Factor Q = (1/R)sqrt(L/C), making the resonance curve sharper.
