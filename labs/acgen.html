Key Formulas & Equations

$$\Phi_B(t) = N B A \cos(\omega t)$$
$$e(t) = -\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt} = N B A \omega \sin(\omega t) = e_0 \sin(\omega t)$$
$$e_0 = N B A \omega = 2\pi f N B A$$

Frequently Asked Conceptual & Viva Questions

When is induced EMF maximum in an AC generator?

When the plane of the coil is parallel to the magnetic field (magnetic flux Phi is zero, but its rate of change dPhi/dt is maximum).

What is the phase difference between magnetic flux and induced EMF?

Exactly 90 degrees (pi/2 radians): flux varies as cos(omega*t) while EMF varies as sin(omega*t).
