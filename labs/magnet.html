Key Formulas & Equations

$$\vec{F} = q(\vec{v} \times \vec{B}) = qvB\sin\theta$$
$$r = \frac{mv}{qB}$$
$$T = \frac{2\pi m}{qB},\quad f_c = \frac{qB}{2\pi m}$$

Frequently Asked Conceptual & Viva Questions

Does a magnetic field change the kinetic energy of a moving charge?

No. Since magnetic force F = q(v x B) is always perpendicular to velocity v, work done is zero and speed stays constant.

What path does a charge follow if it enters B at an oblique angle?

It follows a helical path whose pitch equals v*cos(theta) * T.
