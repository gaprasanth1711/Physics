Frequently Asked Conceptual & Viva Questions

What happens when impact parameter b approaches zero?

The alpha particle undergoes a head-on collision and rebounds back at scattering angle theta = 180 degrees at the distance of closest approach r0.

Why did Rutherford conclude the atom is mostly empty space?

Because over 99.8% of alpha particles passed straight through the thin gold foil with minimal deflection.
