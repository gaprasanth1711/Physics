Frequently Asked Conceptual & Viva Questions

What happens to fringe width if slit separation d increases?

Since beta = lambda*D/d, increasing slit spacing d makes interference fringes narrower (closer together).

What happens if the YDSE apparatus is immersed in water?

Wavelength decreases to lambda/mu, so fringe width shrinks to beta/mu.
