Frequently Asked Conceptual & Viva Questions

What is the shape of a wavefront from a distant star or point source at large distance?

Plane wavefront (whereas nearby point sources form spherical wavefronts, and line slits form cylindrical wavefronts).

Why is the angle between rays and wavefronts always 90 degrees?

Rays indicate direction of energy propagation perpendicular to surfaces of constant phase (wavefronts).
