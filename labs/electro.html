Key Formulas & Equations

$$F = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2}$$
$$\vec{E} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{q}{r^2}\hat{r}$$
$$E_{\text{axial}} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{2p}{r^3},\quad E_{\text{eq}} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{p}{r^3}$$

Frequently Asked Conceptual & Viva Questions

Why do two electric field lines never intersect?

If they crossed, the net electric field at the point of intersection would have two different directions, which is physically impossible.

What is the ratio of axial to equatorial field at equal large distance?

For r >> a, E(axial) = 2 * E(equatorial) and their directions are antiparallel.
